⚠️ Safety Alert — When to Seek Immediate Medical Review

Brandt-Daroff exercises induce transient vertigo. However, stop immediately and contact emergency medical services if you notice any of the following neurological symptoms:

  • Sudden weakness or drooping in your face, arm, or leg
  • Numbness, loss of sensation, or tingling on one side of your body
  • Acute vision changes (double vision, partial blindness)
  • Slurred speech or severe loss of balance/coordination

What is BPPV (Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo)?

BPPV is one of the most common causes of vertigo. It occurs when microscopic calcium carbonate crystals (otoconia) dislodge from their normal position in the utricle and migrate into one of the fluid-filled semicircular canals of the inner ear.

When you tilt or move your head, these loose crystals shift within the canal fluid, stimulating vestibular nerve endings and causing rapid, intense spinning episodes (vertigo) that typically last 15 to 45 seconds.

What is the Brandt-Daroff Method?

The Brandt-Daroff method is a series of lateral positional movements designed for home self-treatment. Through systematic changes in posture, it helps disperse and dislodge otoconial particles back into the vestibule where they can be naturally reabsorbed by inner ear cells.

In addition, the repetition trains the central nervous system to compensate for abnormal vestibular signaling (central vestibular habituation). Clinical studies report a success rate of approximately 95% when practiced consistently.

How a Cycle Works (The 5 Steps)

A complete treatment session consists of 5 consecutive cycles. Each cycle includes the following five positions:

Step 1 · Starting Position Transition

Sit upright on the edge of your bed, looking straight ahead. Ensure the area around the bed is clear.

Step 2 · Lying on Right Side 30 seconds

Lie down smoothly onto your right side while turning your head 45° upward toward the ceiling. Hold for 30 seconds or until spinning ceases.

Step 3 · Return to Sitting 30 seconds rest

Sit back up to the upright starting position on the edge of the bed. Rest for 30 seconds.

Step 4 · Lying on Left Side 30 seconds

Lie down smoothly onto your left side while turning your head 45° upward toward the ceiling. Hold for 30 seconds or until spinning ceases.

Step 5 · Inter-Cycle Rest 2 minutes

Return to upright sitting and rest for 2 full minutes before starting the next cycle. This prevents nausea and resets inner ear fluids.

Frequency, Duration & Practical Tips

Recommended Protocol: 3 sessions per day (morning, midday, evening) for approximately 14 days. Continue until you remain completely free of dizziness for two consecutive days.

Key Tips:

  • Perform the exercises next to a bed or firm surface.
  • Move smoothly and deliberately; do not jerk or wrench your neck.
  • Mild to moderate dizziness during early days is expected — it confirms particles are moving.
  • Consistency is essential: do not skip sessions even if you feel improved after 3 or 4 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for Brandt-Daroff exercises to work?

Most patients begin noticing meaningful symptom reduction within 3 to 5 days. Complete resolution is typically achieved within 10 to 14 days of compliant practice.
